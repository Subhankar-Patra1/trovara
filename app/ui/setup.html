In the search panel

Enter
opens the file (at the page or moment, where its app can)
Ctrl+Enter
shows it in Explorer
Ctrl+C
copies the file, to paste anywhere
Ctrl+O
opens the search window: more results, a grid for pictures
↑ ↓
move between results
Esc
closes the panel

Catch up fast after a restart

While Trovara runs it sees every change at once. When it starts, it has to find what changed while it was off: by looking through every folder (a few seconds to minutes), or, with fast catch-up, by asking Windows' change journal (well under a second).
